# Task prompt: BRAND round 2 → ui-ux-designer (more creative logo concepts)

## 1. Role and seniority
You are the same principal brand and identity designer as in round 1: 18 years on global fintech and proptech identities, known for one clever geometric idea per mark.

## 2. Context
- Binding brief: `docs/design/brand/BRAND_IDENTITY_BRIEF.md`. Your round 1 work is in `docs/design/brand/`.
- **Stakeholder feedback on round 1 (2026-11-03):** none of A Seked Ladder, B Obelisk Line or C The Ascent is approved. The stakeholder asked for **"more options, be creative"**. Round 1 stayed too close to one idea: a pyramid or capstone, plus steps or a tower. Push much further while keeping the brief's rules: minimal, flat, ownable, works at 16 px, no tourist pyramid, no house, no gavel.

## 3. Task
Explore **8 fresh concepts**, each from a different territory. Do not reuse the round 1 forms. Use these territories (one or two concepts each), or better ones you find:
1. **Bennu bird / phoenix:** in Heliopolis myth the Bennu (a heron) rose from the Benben stone at creation. A rising bird or wing built from capstone geometry: rebirth, rising value.
2. **The first sunrise:** the Benben caught the first light of creation. A sun disk or ray meeting a gilded apex; a horizon line read as a price chart.
3. **Letterform monogram "B" or "BB":** two B counters stacked as floors of a tower, or the hyphen in BEN-BEN as the bid step, mirrored to form a symmetrical mark.
4. **Arabic "ب" (ba)** blended with the Latin B or with an apex: a bilingual mark that feels Egyptian without Pharaonic imagery.
5. **Negative space:** a tower, keyhole or door formed in the gap between two shapes; an arrow or "up" tick hidden in the counter.
6. **Time and auction:** a 24-hour dial or countdown arc whose segment forms an apex or tower; the idea that the auction is time.
7. **The primordial mound / origin:** an abstract rising mound or "first ground" as a single confident curve or facet. Property begins with land.
8. **A wildcard:** the most surprising idea you can defend.

For each concept:
- a name;
- the idea in one sentence;
- the story link to Benben, real estate and auctions;
- a hand-built SVG in navy and gold on ivory, and on navy;
- an icon test at 60, 29 and 16 px;
- a risk note (what it could be mistaken for);
- an honest score out of 5 for originality, legibility at small size, premium feel and fit with the brief.

Then shortlist your **top 3** with reasons. Do not refine them yet: the stakeholder picks first.

## 4. Deliverables
- `docs/design/brand/round-2/concept-<n>-<name>.svg` (8 files; an on-navy variant inside the same file or as a separate file)
- `docs/design/brand/round-2/ROUND-2.md` (all 8, the scores, the shortlist)
- `docs/design/brand/round-2/round-2-presentation.html`: one clean page with all 8 on a grid at large size, their small-size tests, the short story under each, and the shortlist highlighted. It must also include a "how it looks as an app icon" row using an iOS-style rounded square. Self-contained; Google Fonts only.
- Keep the round 1 files untouched. Use the wordmark "BEN-BEN" exactly in any lockup preview.

## 5. Constraints
- Flat colour, few paths, no gradients except an optional subtle one for marketing only.
- Do not run git commands. Work only in `docs/design/brand/round-2/`.

## 6. Self-check
- [ ] All 8 concepts come from different territories and look different from each other and from round 1.
- [ ] Each concept was viewed at 16 px; failures are noted honestly.
- [ ] No house, gavel or literal pyramid illustration.

## 7. Report
The 8 names with one line each, the top 3 and why, and any questions for the stakeholder.
