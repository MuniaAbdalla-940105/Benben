AUTH-01 Phone OTP sign-up and login

Phone OTP is the only login (Q-40). Full-page version, opened from the Profile tab or a deep link; inside Join to Bid the same PhoneStep and OtpStep render in the AU-04 sheet. Calls: signInWithOtp({ phone }), verifyOtp({ phone, token, type: 'sms' }). After success the user returns to where they came from. Staging: the mock SMS adapter writes to the QA inbox.

Components

  • AuthPage · BrandMark · PhoneStep (PhoneField) · OtpStep (OtpInput, ResendTimer) · InlineAlert

Input rules

  • Egyptian mobile: 10, 11, 12 or 15 prefix, 10 digits after +20; accepts Latin and Arabic-Indic digits and a leading 0.
  • 6-digit code, autocomplete="one-time-code", paste fills all boxes, auto-submit on the 6th digit.
  • Resend after 60 s.

Responsive

  • 768+: centred card 420 px on ivory, brand lockup above.
