AU-04 Join to Bid · growing bottom sheet (UX 1A)

The auction stays visible above the sheet. Steps: login (in sheet) → KYC T1 (the only full-screen step, KYC-01) → terms + lock (in sheet). The sheet is a money moment: no motion, no red except errors, one primary action, receipt-style summary. Calls: join_auction (wallet), payments-create-intent purpose join_lock (pay the shortfall), then join_after_payment on webhook. Contract ST-008 / ST-007.

Components

  • JoinToBidSheet (BottomSheet) · JoinStepper · AuctionContextRow
  • PhoneStep (PhoneField, OtpInput; reuses AUTH-01 parts)
  • MoneySummary variant=lock · DepositOutcomes · WinCostPreview
  • PaymentMethodPicker (RadioCard) · TermsCheckbox · LockDepositButton
  • JoinReceipt (success) · InlineAlert for errors

Rules shown

  • Amount from auctions.deposit_amount (default 5,000). One lock per auction; a second auction needs a second lock.
  • CTA disabled until the T&C box is ticked. Acceptance logs version, time, IP, device.
  • Available ≥ deposit → wallet lock. Less → pay only the shortfall; lock happens after the webhook, never on redirect.
  • Nothing is charged before the tap; the button repeats the amount.

Responsive

  • 375: sheet up to 92% height, auction strip visible on top.
  • 768+: right-side (inline-end) drawer 440 px wide beside AU-02; same content.
  • Focus is trapped in the sheet and returns to "Join to Bid" on close.
