SHELL App shell, bottom navigation and preferences

Arabic RTL by default; Auctions is the default, centred and emphasised tab. Five tabs (PRD 3.1): Explore · Rent · Auctions · Requests · Profile/Wallet. Rent and Requests show "Coming soon" in Phase 1 (Q-83). Theme follows the phone (ADR-003 §3); language, digits and outbid sound live in the preferences sheet. Brand mark from docs/design/brand/logo/ only.

Components

  • AppShell · AppTopBar · BrandMark · BottomNav (5 NavItem) · DesktopTopNav
  • PreferencesSheet (LanguageSwitch, DigitsSwitch, SoundSwitch, ThemeInfo)
  • ComingSoonPage · OfflineBanner · SplashScreen · ErrorBoundaryPage

Formatting (ST-002)

  • Money: piasters → whole EGP, thousands separators, never floats. AR: 8,450,000 ج.م · EN: EGP 8,450,000.
  • Digits: Latin default; Arabic-Indic toggle changes all numbers, dates and timers (try it in the bar above).
  • Dates and times: Africa/Cairo.
  • Inputs accept both digit sets.

Breakpoints

  • 375–767: top bar + bottom nav (72 px + safe area).
  • 768–1279: bottom nav stays; content 2 columns.
  • 1280+: top navigation, no bottom nav, max width 1200.

Main frames
