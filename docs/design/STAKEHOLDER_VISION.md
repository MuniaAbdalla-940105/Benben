## The design tension to solve
"Live & energetic" plus "everyday first-time bidders" plus "EGP 5,000 deposits" means the excitement must never cost trust. Every direction must show:
- where energy lives (live auctions, countdowns, bids, Just Sold);
- where it calms down (deposit lock, payment, KYC, T&Cs, winner obligations);

and must stay WCAG 2.2 AA, including with motion reduced.
