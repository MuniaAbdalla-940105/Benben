تواصل مع الدعم

Motion and LIVE language

  • LIVE pulse: one soft red ring, 1.6 s. Reduced: static ring.
  • New bid: price rolls up 300 ms; the bid history row slides in. Reduced: fade.
  • Time bar drains from the start edge (right in Arabic) under the header.
  • Final 5 minutes: the header fact strip turns red, timer grows to 28 px, label "الدقائق الأخيرة". No blinking.
  • "+3:00": a chip in the header ("+3:00 تم التمديد · 2") plus a polite toast, 4 s.

Accessibility and RTL notes

  • Gold is never used for text on light surfaces except Gold 700 (5.4:1).
  • Focus ring shown on "انضم للمزايدة" (navy, 2 px, offset 3 px).
  • Stepper and progress bars run right-to-left in Arabic, left-to-right in English.
  • Risk: may feel too quiet for "live & energetic" if live tiles are not used generously on Home.
