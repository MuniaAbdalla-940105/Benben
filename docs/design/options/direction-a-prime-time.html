تواصل مع الدعم

Motion and LIVE language

  • Bid ticker crawls in reading direction (right-to-left in Arabic). Reduced motion: static "last bid" line.
  • Price update: split-flap digit flip 180 ms, gold underline flash. Reduced: instant swap, underline only.
  • LIVE: 1 Hz blink (below 3 flashes/s). Reduced: solid dot.
  • "+3:00 Extended": full-width bar slams in under the scorebug, stays 4 s, then collapses into an "Extensions 3" counter.
  • Money sheets: no motion except the sheet open (200 ms ease-out).

Accessibility and RTL notes

  • Timer state has a label and icon, not just colour ("ينتهي قريبًا", "الدقائق الأخيرة").
  • Focus ring shown on "انضم للمزايدة" (gold on navy, 11.3:1).
  • Back chevron and list chevrons mirrored; clock and play icons not mirrored; progress fills from the right.
  • Risk: dark + neon-like accents and a ticker can read as a betting app to first-time bidders. Gold instead of lime keeps it premium, but the risk stays.
