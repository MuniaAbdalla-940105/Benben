تواصل مع الدعم

Motion and LIVE language

  • Gold ring sweeps the remaining time; turns amber under 1h and red under 5 min, with a text label.
  • The call (final 60 s): "مرة" at 60 s, "مرتين" at 30 s, "بيع" at 0. A new bid resets it and shows "+3:00". Reduced motion: no breathing, steps still change.
  • Lot reveal: hero cross-fades 600 ms between lots; no auto-advance while the user reads (WCAG 2.2.2).
  • Sold moment: the price settles in gold with "بيع"; no confetti.

Accessibility and RTL notes

  • Ring progress runs counter-clockwise in Arabic (mirrored), the clock digits are not mirrored.
  • The 52° slope corner is cut on the start-top side and mirrors with direction.
  • Kufi needs 1.8 line height for body; display headlines are capped at 2 lines on 375 px.
  • Risk: duotone tint hides true colours of finishes. Gallery opens in natural colour; tint is only on the stage.
  • Focus ring shown on the CTA and the T&C checkbox.
