# BEN-BEN brand identity brief (stakeholder, 2026-11-02)

Given by Munia Abdalla (stakeholder). This is binding, verbatim input and supersedes "Brand assets: nothing yet" in `STAKEHOLDER_VISION.md`.

---

Create a world-class, iconic brand identity and app logo for BEN-BEN, a premium real estate auction platform.

THE CREATIVE CONCEPT: "ANCIENT VALUE. MODERN LEGACY."

The name BEN-BEN is inspired by ancient Egyptian mythology and the Benben stone, a sacred primordial symbol associated with creation, origin, and the beginning of existence.

Transform this historical meaning into a powerful modern visual identity that connects ancient Egyptian heritage with the future of real estate investment and digital property auctions.

CORE LOGO IDEA:

Create a sophisticated, abstract geometric symbol inspired by the ancient Benben stone — a distinctive pyramid-like or rising architectural form. Seamlessly transform this shape into a modern skyscraper or premium real estate tower, symbolizing the evolution of property and wealth through time.

Integrate a subtle auction concept into the geometry, perhaps through a rising bid indicator, an elegant ascending line, or a dynamic upward movement. The auction element should feel intelligently embedded rather than represented by a literal judge's gavel.

VISUAL DIRECTION:

* Minimalist, bold, futuristic, and instantly recognizable.
* Premium luxury-tech aesthetic.
* Strong geometric architecture inspired by ancient Egyptian proportions.
* A subtle reference to Egyptian heritage without looking like a tourism logo or traditional Pharaonic souvenir.
* Elegant negative space and clever visual symbolism.
* A timeless design suitable for a serious real estate investment platform.

COLOR PALETTE:

* Deep midnight navy or sophisticated charcoal.
* Champagne gold or brushed metallic gold.
* Optional warm ivory accents.
* High contrast, elegant, and premium.

TYPOGRAPHY:

The brand name must read exactly:

BEN-BEN

Use a custom geometric sans-serif typeface with distinctive letterforms. The typography should feel modern, confident, and globally recognizable.

Optional Arabic subtitle:
"مزاد عقارات"

IMPORTANT DESIGN REQUIREMENTS:

1. The symbol must work independently as an iOS and Android application icon.
2. The logo must remain recognizable at very small sizes.
3. Avoid generic real estate icons, ordinary house outlines, literal auction hammers, and overused pyramid illustrations.
4. Avoid excessive 3D effects, unnecessary gradients, and complicated details.
5. Focus on a genuinely original visual metaphor that connects the ancient Benben stone with modern real estate auctions.
6. Make the result look like the identity of a billion-dollar international proptech company.

FINAL OUTPUT:
Present a stunning, professionally art-directed logo with a standalone app icon, the BEN-BEN wordmark, and a refined brand presentation on a clean background. Prioritize originality, simplicity, symbolism, and memorability.
