BEN-BEN · Logo round 2 · 3 November 2026

Eight new ways to say
"Ancient Value. Modern Legacy."

Round 1 stayed inside one idea: the capstone. Round 2 goes to eight territories: the Bennu bird, the first sunrise, a Latin monogram, the Arabic ب, negative space, time, the primordial mound and a wildcard from the name itself. Each is two or three flat shapes, shown on ivory and on navy, and tested at 60, 29 and 16 px. The three shortlisted concepts are outlined in gold. They are not refined yet: you pick first.

All eight concepts

Concepts

01 · Bennu bird

Bennu Rising

Two wings swept up at the capstone slope lift a gold diamond body: the Bennu taking flight from the Benben.

In the Heliopolis myth the Bennu heron rose from the Benben stone at creation and returns to renew it. The bird is rebirth and rising value; the wings are cut at 51.84°, so the stone is inside the bird.

Could be mistaken for: Can read as a letter W, a generic eagle or military insignia, or a dragonfly. Close to some car and airline badges.

02 · The first sunrise

First Light

A gold sun disk rises behind a horizon that steps up once: the first light of creation over a rising price line.

The Benben was the first ground to catch the sun. The horizon is land (real estate) and its single step is the bid; the disk is the gilded apex turned into light.

Could be mistaken for: Sunset over stairs: travel, beach-resort and solar-energy logos use the same parts. At 16 px it holds as a disk over a bar.

03 · Latin monogram

B Spire

A geometric lowercase b whose stem rises past its bowls into a gold spire cut at the seked.

B for BEN-BEN; the bowls stacked on the stem read as floors of a building, and the gold spire is the capstone on the modern tower.

Could be mistaken for: The gold tip can read as a pencil or an accent mark (b with an acute). The counters close up at 16 px, where it becomes a plain b.

04 · Arabic ب

Ba Nuqta

A Kufi ب (ba) with its dot drawn as a gold rhombus: the calligrapher's nuqta as the point of value.

ب begins بن بن, بيت (home) and بيع (sale). In Arabic calligraphy the rhombic nuqta is the unit that sets every proportion, as the seked did for the pyramids. Egyptian and Arab without any Pharaonic image.

Could be mistaken for: Latin readers may see a tray, a shelf, a bracket or a "U" lying down. It has no built-in rising idea, so the auction story is carried by the brand rather than the mark.

05 · Negative space

The Opening

A navy block with a tower-shaped opening, and a gold tower standing in it: a door, a keyhole and a skyline in one cut.

The opening is the way into property; the tower inside it is the asset you win. The apex of the opening uses the Benben angle.

Could be mistaken for: A pointed doorway reads as a church or mosque window, or a castle door. It sits close to the "house" family we were asked to avoid.

06 · Time and auction

Golden Hour

A 24-hour dial with a gold wedge at 12 o'clock whose opening is exactly 76.3°, the apex angle of the Great Pyramid.

Every BEN-BEN auction lasts 24 hours: the auction is time. The wedge is both the last hour on the dial and a capstone seen from above, hidden in plain sight.

Could be mistaken for: Can read as a pie chart, a timer, a target or a map pin. At 16 px the ring and wedge hold, but the 76.3° story needs explaining.

07 · Primordial mound

First Ground

A single gold mound rises asymmetrically out of navy water lines: the first ground, already climbing to the right.

The Benben is the primordial mound that rose from the waters of Nun. Property begins with land; the mound's slope climbs left to right like a price.

Could be mistaken for: Reads as a sunset, a cloud, a loaf of bread or a hamburger. The two water lines merge at 16 px. This is the weakest small-size result.

08 · Wildcard

The Hyphen

The name as a mark: two buildings, BEN and BEN, joined by the gold hyphen as a rising bridge.

BEN-BEN is two equal words joined by a hyphen; here they become a seller's building and a buyer's taller one, and the gold step between them is the deal. It ties straight to the wordmark's gold hyphen.

Could be mistaken for: Can read as a bar chart or a stats icon. Also considered and dropped: a raised bid paddle, which read as a lollipop or magnifier at 16 px.

Shortlist

Top 3, not yet refined

#1 · concept 04

Ba Nuqta

The most ownable mark in the set and the best at 16 px. It is the only concept that is unmistakably Egyptian and Arab without a Pharaonic image, and ب links BEN-BEN, بيت (home) and بيع (sale). It needs the brand system (gold hyphen, LIVE moments) to carry the auction story.

#2 · concept 06

Golden Hour

The strongest auction idea: the 24-hour auction is the product, and the dial says it. The 76.3° wedge hides the pyramid where only the curious will find it, which keeps it far from tourism. It also gives an ownable countdown motif for the app.

#3 · concept 08

The Hyphen

It comes straight from the name and the existing gold hyphen, so the mark and the wordmark become one system. It tells buyer, seller and the rising deal in three flat shapes. The bar-chart risk is the trade-off.
