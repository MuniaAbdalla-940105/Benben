01 · The concept

The first stone. The next tower. The rising bid.

In the Heliopolis creation myth the Benben is the first mound of land to rise from the waters of Nun: the origin of all ground, and the shape of every pyramid's gilded capstone. BEN-BEN turns that first piece of ground into the modern property market, where value is found in public, in 24 hours, one step at a time.

01 · ANCIENT VALUE

The capstone

We start from the Benben capstone, drawn at the true 51.84° slope of the Great Pyramid.

02 · MODERN FORM

Raised and split into two buildings

The capstone becomes the crown of a building, and one vertical cut turns it into two slabs with sloped crowns: architecture, not a monument.

03 · MODERN LEGACY

One half rises, in gold

The tower half lifts exactly one step above the stone. That step is the bid: every auction moves value up from where it started.

03 · Construction

Built on the seked, not on taste

  • Slope 51.84°, a 14 : 11 rise to run.The Egyptian builders' seked of 5½ palms per cubit, the slope of the Great Pyramid. Each half is 22 units wide and its crown rises 28 units.
  • Step = half the crown (14 units).The tower half is lifted by exactly one half-crown. Smaller reads as a mistake; larger breaks the capstone memory.
  • Gap 4 units (1/12 of the width).Wide enough to survive at 29 px; the 16 px favicon widens it to a full pixel.
  • Bounding box 48 × 72 = 2 : 3.Two flat shapes, eight points in total, no curves. The silhouette is a capstone with its right half rising.
  • Colour logic.The stone half is ivory (or navy on light grounds); the lifted tower is always gold: the capstone of the Benben was gilded.

05 · Wordmark

BEN-BEN, drawn, not typed

A custom geometric sans built as outlines on a 100-unit cap grid (stems 18, bars 16). No font needs to be installed. It was drawn from scratch; Outfit (Google Fonts) was the reference for proportion and is the brand's companion text face.

Seked-cut E

Every arm ends on the 51.84° slope, parallel to the stone's rising face.

Shouldered N

The left shoulder of each N is cut at the seked, a miniature of the symbol's stone half.

The gold step

The hyphen is a gold parallelogram with seked ends: the bid step between the old name and the new.

06 · Arabic subtitle

مزاد عقارات, in Readex Pro

Readex Pro (Google Fonts, medium 500) is a geometric Arabic designed as a companion to the Lexend Latin family: monoline strokes, open counters, a low-contrast Kufi-leaning skeleton. It shares the wordmark's even stroke and flat terminals, so the two scripts feel like one voice.

Set at 56 % of the wordmark width, centred, between two gold rules. Outlined in the SVG files. In the app, Readex Pro is also the recommended Arabic UI face.
