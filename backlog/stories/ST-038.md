## Story
As a buyer, I want to report a fake or wrong listing, and as Trust & Safety I want reports to hide and punish fakes quickly, so that the Marketplace stays trustworthy.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given MK-06, when a logged-in user reports, then they pick a reason (unit doesn't exist, already sold/rented, wrong price, wrong photos, broker posing as owner, scam / asked for money upfront, other), add details and evidence.
- Given 3 independent reports in 7 days, when the third arrives, then the listing is auto-hidden pending review with a 24 h SLA (PRD 5.3).
- Given reports, when reviewed, then they appear in the Approval Queue "Reports" tab. Assumption (Q-68): AD-10 is not in the MVP, so reports go through AD-16/17.
- Given a reviewer decision (F5), when Dismiss, Hide, Remove + strike, or Ban is chosen, then the reporter and lister are notified.
- Given strikes, when confirmed fake, then 1 strike + listing removed; 2 strikes = 30-day posting ban; 3 strikes or any scam = permanent ban (PRD 5.3). Permanent ban needs an Ops Supervisor approval (PRD 8.1.4).
- Given a paid boost on a listing proven fake (M5), when this story ships, then boosts are not in the MVP (Decision Q-53).
- Given a malicious reporter, when reports are dismissed repeatedly, then this is recorded. Assumption (Q-58): no trust score in the MVP.
- Given a banned user, when they try to bid or list anywhere, then they are blocked (PRD 8.2 shared bans).
