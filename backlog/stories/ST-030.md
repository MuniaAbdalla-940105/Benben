## Story
As Benben, I want a winner who misses the 48 hours to forfeit their deposit and the unit to be offered to the next bidder, so that serious buyers get the property and the deposit deters fake wins.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given `deadline_at` passes without completion and no approved extension, when the deadline job runs, then the auction becomes WINNER_DEFAULTED, the winner's lock is forfeited to the Benben revenue account (PRD 2.3 rule 6, A10), the user gets a default strike, and they are notified.
- Given a default, when the second-chance step runs, then rank 2 gets an offer at their own highest placed bid with a 24 h expiry, and their lock stays held (PRD 3.7 step 2). Assumption (Q-28).
- Given AU-08, when rank 2 opens it, then they see the offer amount, a 24 h server-time timer and Accept / Decline.
- Given Accept, when tapped, then rank 2 becomes the winner with a fresh 48 h obligation (SECOND_CHANCE → AWAITING_WINNER flow).
- Given Decline or expiry, when it happens, then rank 2's lock is released with no forfeit and the offer goes to rank 3 (PRD 3.7 step 3).
- Given a second-chance winner who then misses their 48 h, when it happens, then it is treated as a winner default (forfeit + strike). Assumption (Q-28).
- Given ranks 2 and 3 decline, or the next bid is below reserve, when it happens, then the seller decision step starts (ST-031) (PRD 3.7 step 4). Assumption (Q-28): below-reserve offers go to the seller first.
- Given a user with 2 defaults in 12 months, when the second strike is added, then bidding is suspended for 6 months (PRD 3.7 step 5). Assumption (Q-30): separate counter from listing strikes.
- Given forfeiture, when it happens, then the T&C acceptance record is linked as evidence (A4).
