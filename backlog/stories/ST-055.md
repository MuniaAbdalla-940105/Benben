## Story
As the stakeholder, I want a production environment and a go-live checklist that shows exactly what is ready and what still blocks the pilot, so that I can make an informed launch decision for New Cairo and Zayed.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given the production Supabase project, when created, then its region and plan follow the decision on Q-06 and Q-44, with migrations applied by the pipeline and no manual edits.
- Given reference data, when loaded, then the area, compound and developer lists for New Cairo and Sheikh Zayed come from an Ops-provided CSV. Assumption (Q-74).
- Given admin users, when seeded, then roles are assigned and 2FA is enforced, with at least two Super Admins (Q-70).
- Given provider adapters, when go-live is assessed, then the checklist shows for each of payments, KYC, SMS/WhatsApp, email whether a real vendor is connected or still a mock (ADR-001).
- Given legal gates, when go-live is assessed, then the checklist lists the status of Q-01, Q-02, Q-03, Q-04, Q-05, Q-06, Q-07, Q-08 and Q-10, each marked done or blocking.
- Given backups and monitoring, when checked, then database backups, error monitoring and the realtime health monitor are active.
- Given the checklist, when complete, then it is shown to the stakeholder for the release decision; only a stakeholder approves go-live.
