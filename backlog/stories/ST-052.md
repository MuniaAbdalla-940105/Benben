## Story
As Benben management, I want one overview of today's auctions and the pilot KPIs, so that I can see platform health at a glance.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given AD-01, when opened, then KPI cards show live auctions, bidders now, today's GMV (hammer prices of completed auctions), deposits locked, pending KYC and open cases (PRD 8.1.3). Open disputes are the manual A13 cases in the MVP (Assumption Q-34).
- Given the alerts feed, when alerts fire (ST-046, ST-049), then they appear newest first with links.
- Given today's auction timeline, when shown, then it lists today's starts and closes in Cairo time.
- Given pilot KPIs (PRD 1.4), when shown, then sell-through rate, 48 h completion rate, verified bidders per auction, hammer vs starting %, and fake listing rate are computed from database views. Assumption (Q-51): no external analytics tool.
- Given roles, when a role lacks access to an underlying module, then its card is hidden.
