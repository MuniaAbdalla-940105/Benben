## Story
As a buyer, I want full details of a listing and a way to save it and ask the lister to call me, so that I can shortlist and act on units I like.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given MK-03, when opened, then it shows gallery, badges, price and payment plan breakdown, specs grid, location, similar listings, a Report button and the sticky contact bar.
- Given badges, when the listing has them, then Verified Owner, Verified Listing and Price Verified show with their meaning (PRD 5.3).
- Given a logged-in user, when they tap Save, then the listing is added to favourites; a guest is asked to log in.
- Given the contact bar, when a logged-in user taps "Request call", then a lead is created and the lister is notified; the buyer's number is not shown in any public field. Assumption (Q-56): request-call lead form only; no in-app chat or masked calls in the MVP (masked calls are out of scope per Decision Q-53).
- Given an expired or removed listing, when opened by a link, then a "No longer available" state shows.
- Given price history and the compare feature, when this story ships, then they are not shown (Decision Q-53).
- Given the "Upcoming auctions like this" rail, when this story ships, then it is not shown; it arrives in ST-048.
