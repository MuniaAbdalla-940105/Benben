## Story
As a user, I want to download my personal data or ask Benben to delete my account, so that my rights under Egypt's data protection law are respected.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given Profile settings, when the user requests an export, then within the configured time they receive a downloadable file (JSON/PDF) of their profile, KYC status (not raw images), listings, auctions, bids and wallet history.
- Given a deletion request, when the user has no active locks, open obligations, pending withdrawals or open cases, then the profile is anonymised and login is disabled; KYC, financial and audit records are kept for the legal retention period. Assumption (Q-41).
- Given open obligations or locks, when deletion is requested, then the user sees what must finish first.
- Given every request, when handled, then it is logged in `audit_logs`.
- Given the privacy notice, when shown, then it explains retention and device-signal use. Assumption (Q-39, Q-41); final text needs counsel (Q-06).
