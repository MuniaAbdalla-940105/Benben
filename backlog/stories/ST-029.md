## Story
As an auction winner, I want a clear checklist and a way to complete each step within 48 hours, so that I secure the property and get my deposit applied to the premium.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given a winner obligation, when created, then it tracks three items: contract signed, down payment + buyer's premium paid, missing T2 documents uploaded (PRD 3.7).
- Given the contract item, when the contract is signed at the Benben office, then a Post-Auction agent uploads the signed scan and marks it signed (PRD F3 step 3). Assumption (Q-04): office signing in the MVP; the e-signature adapter is not used yet.
- Given the payment item, when the winner pays by bank transfer to the escrow account and uploads proof, then Finance confirms it in the Post-Auction Desk. Assumption (Q-11, Q-10).
- Given the T2 item, when the winner is already T2, then it is auto-ticked; otherwise it links to ST-021.
- Given all three items done before `deadline_at`, when the last one completes, then the auction becomes COMPLETED, the EGP 5,000 lock is applied to the buyer's premium, and backup locks are released (PRD 3.7, F3 step 4). Assumption (Q-15): the rest of the premium is collected with the down payment; any excess is released.
- Given the deadline, when it is computed, then it is calendar hours in Africa/Cairo. Assumption (Q-29).
- Given the winner's AU-07 screen, when items complete, then the checklist updates live.
- Given seller payout, when the auction completes, then the escrow release is recorded only; no payout runs through Benben. Assumption (Q-17).
