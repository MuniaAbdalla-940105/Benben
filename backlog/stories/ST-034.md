## Story
As a Content Reviewer, I want one queue of submissions with automatic checks already run, so that I can approve good content fast and stop bad content before it goes public.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given AD-16, when opened, then it lists submissions with type tabs (Marketplace and Reports in the MVP), filters (area, submitter type, SLA, risk score) and SLA and risk badges.
- Given an item, when listed, then it shows automatic checks: KYC status, duplicate match, price outlier, image quality, banned words or phone numbers in text, and strikes (PRD 8.1.5 B). Assumption (Q-58): no trust score in the MVP.
- Given AD-17, when a reviewer opens an item, then they see submitted data | app preview | documents side by side and can comment on specific fields.
- Given a decision, when the reviewer chooses Approve, Request changes (field comments), Reject (reason code + note) or Escalate, then the status updates and the customer is notified automatically.
- Given Approve on a Marketplace listing, when saved, then it is published with `expires_at = now + 30 days`.
- Given a reviewer, when they try to approve their own submission, then it is blocked (C7).
- Given an item past its SLA (C8), when the SLA passes, then it escalates to a supervisor, moves to the top and the customer gets a "taking longer" notice.
- Given bulk approve, when items are selected, then only items with all checks green can be approved in bulk.
- Given auto-approve, when configured, then it is off for Marketplace in the MVP. Assumption (Q-58).
- Given rejection reason codes, when shown, then they match PRD 8.1.5 (incomplete data, poor/fake photos, duplicate, unrealistic price, missing/invalid documents, ownership not proven, contact info in text, prohibited content, other).
