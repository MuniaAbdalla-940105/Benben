## Story
As a participant, I want to see right away whether I won, lost or am a backup, and track all my auctions in one place, so that I know what to do next and where my deposit is.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given the auction ends, when a winner opens AU-07, then they see a 48 h countdown (server time), the checklist (contract, down payment + buyer's premium, missing T2 documents) and the contract CTA (PRD 3.4 step 6, 3.7).
- Given a non-winner, when they open AU-07, then they see "Deposit released"; a rank 2–3 bidder sees "Held as backup bidder" with an explanation.
- Given a guest or non-participant, when they open the ended auction, then they see the hammer price (or "No sale") but no personal outcome.
- Given AU-09, when opened, then tabs Watching · Bidding · Won · Lost list the user's auctions with the deposit status per auction (active, backup, released, forfeited, applied).
- Given an empty tab, when opened, then the empty state links to Auctions Home.
