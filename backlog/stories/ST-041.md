## Story
As a bidder, I want timely alerts about my auctions and my deposit, so that I can act before it is too late.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given a "Remind me" on a scheduled auction, when start is 1 h and 5 min away, then push is sent (PRD 3.1, 8.3).
- Given an outbid, when it happens, then push is sent instantly; in the last 10 minutes SMS is also sent (PRD 8.3).
- Given an extension, when it happens, then it is in-app only.
- Given a win, when the auction closes, then push, SMS, WhatsApp and email are sent; 48 h reminders go at close, +12 h, +24 h, +36 h and +44 h on push, SMS and WhatsApp (PRD 3.7, 8.3).
- Given a deposit lock, release or forfeiture, when it happens, then push, SMS and email are sent.
- Given a second-chance offer, when sent, then push, SMS, WhatsApp and email are sent.
- Given a listing reported or removed, when decided, then push and email are sent.
- Given a completed obligation, when the deadline reminders are due, then no further reminders are sent.
- Given duplicate triggers for the same event, when they fire, then one notification per channel is sent (idempotent).
