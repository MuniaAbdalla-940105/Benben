## Story
As the stakeholder, I want early proof that our stack can broadcast bids to 5,000 viewers of one auction within 300 ms, so that we find out in sprint 1 whether a dedicated realtime service is needed.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given a staging auction, when a load tool opens 5,000 concurrent realtime subscriptions (mostly anonymous viewers) on its channel, then connections are held for at least 15 minutes and the connection success rate is recorded.
- Given that load, when bids are placed through the real `place_bid` function at a realistic rate (including a final-minute burst), then the time from bid commit to receipt by subscribers is measured and p50/p95/p99 are reported.
- Given the results, when p95 < 300 ms and no bid is lost or misordered, then the spike passes and the result is linked in ADR-001.
- Given p95 ≥ 300 ms or dropped messages, when the spike fails, then the solution-architect drafts a follow-up ADR for a dedicated bid/realtime service and the stakeholder is told in the sprint review (ADR-001).
- Given plan quotas, when the test runs, then the Supabase plan limits used are recorded, plus the plan needed for pilot sizing. Assumption (Q-45): up to 10 LIVE auctions and 10,000 total connections in the pilot.
- Given test traffic, when it runs, then it uses only synthetic users and data.
