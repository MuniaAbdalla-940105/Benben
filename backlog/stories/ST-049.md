## Story
As a bidder, I want auctions to pause automatically when the platform's live connection fails, so that nobody wins or loses because of an outage.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given a server-side heartbeat probe, when it publishes and receives on the realtime layer, then its success and latency are recorded continuously.
- Given the probe fails for more than 30 s during LIVE, when detected, then all affected LIVE/EXTENDED auctions become PAUSED and an incident banner appears in the Control Room (A7). Assumption (Q-26): a platform-wide failure pauses all live auctions.
- Given the probe recovers, when Auction Control resumes (or auto-resume if configured), then `ends_at` is extended by the outage duration + 5 min (A7) and participants are notified.
- Given websocket error rate > 2% or gateway down, when detected, then an alert fires in AD-02 (PRD F2 step 2).
- Given a pause, when bidders reconnect, then they see the "Paused" banner and the resumed end time.
