## Story
As a bidder, I want my deposit released the moment I lose, or held only while I am a backup, so that my money is never stuck without reason.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given an auction reaches ENDED, when the outcome is computed, then bidders are ranked by distinct user on their highest valid bid. Assumption (Q-28).
- Given AWAITING_WINNER, when set, then a `winner_obligations` row is created for rank 1 with `deadline_at = close + 48 h`, and the participant status becomes `winner`.
- Given ranks 2 and 3, when the auction closes, then their locks stay active and their status is `backup` (PRD 2.3 rule 4). Assumption (Q-27): held until their place in the second-chance chain is resolved, not a fixed 72 h.
- Given all other participants (including those who joined but never bid), when the auction closes, then their locks are released to Available instantly and their status is `released` (PRD 2.3 rule 4).
- Given NO_BIDS, when set, then all locks are released.
- Given RESERVE_NOT_MET, when set, then the top bidder's lock is held during the seller's 24 h decision (A9); ranks 2–3 stay as backups. Assumption (Q-27).
- Given a user who won two auctions (A15), when both close, then each has its own lock and its own 48 h obligation with no cross-netting.
- Given every release, when done, then it is a ledger transaction and an `auction_events` row, and the user is notified (ST-041).
- Given the close job runs twice, when it repeats, then no lock is released twice (idempotent).
