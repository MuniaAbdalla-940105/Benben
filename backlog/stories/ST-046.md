## Story
As Benben, I want to stop sellers and their linked accounts from bidding on their own auctions, so that prices are real and bidders can trust them.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given account signals, when a user signs up, does KYC, pays or logs in, then National ID, phone, payment card fingerprint (from the gateway) and a first-party device ID are stored as linkage signals. Assumption (Q-39): no third-party fingerprint vendor; consent is covered in the privacy notice.
- Given a seller's auction, when an account sharing any strong signal (National ID, phone, card fingerprint) with the seller tries to join, then joining is refused (PRD 2.1).
- Given a weaker shared signal (device ID), when a user joins, then they can join but Auction Control gets an alert "possible linked account" (PRD F2 step 2).
- Given a bid spike from linked accounts, when detected, then an alert fires in AD-02.
- Given confirmed shill bidding (A12), when Auction Control acts, then offending bids are voided (ST-026), the auction may be cancelled, and both accounts are banned (permanent ban needs an Ops Supervisor approval).
- Given AD-09, when a reviewer opens a case, then linked accounts are listed (feeds ST-022).
