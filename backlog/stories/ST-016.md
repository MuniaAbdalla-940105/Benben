## Story
As a guest or user, I want the app to open on live and upcoming auctions, so that I immediately see what I can bid on now and soon.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given the app opens, when AU-01 loads, then modules appear top to bottom: Live Now hero carousel (cover, current bid, bidder count, red LIVE badge, big countdown), Ending Soon (< 1 h, pulsing timer), Starting Soon (with "Remind me" bell), Auction Calendar (week view by area), Just Sold (hammer vs starting price) (PRD 3.1).
- Given area filter chips, when the user picks New Cairo or Sheikh Zayed, then all rails filter to that area. Assumption (Q-74): pilot areas only.
- Given a logged-in user taps "Remind me", when saved, then a reminder for 1 h and 5 min before start is stored and the auction appears under Watching in My Auctions (delivery of the push is ST-041).
- Given a guest taps "Remind me", when tapped, then they are asked to log in (PRD 2.1).
- Given live prices change, when bids are broadcast, then hero and rail cards update without reload.
- Given no auctions in a module, when rendered, then the module shows its empty state or is hidden, per design.
- Given the "For you" module, when this story ships, then it is not shown; it arrives in ST-048.
- Given a 4G profile, when AU-01 loads, then first paint is < 2 s and images are WebP/AVIF from the CDN (PRD 8.4).
