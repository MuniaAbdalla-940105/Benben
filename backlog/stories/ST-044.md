## Story
As a Finance officer, I want to handle duplicate-charge refunds, chargebacks and forfeiture reversals with evidence and approvals, so that money disputes are fair and controlled.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given a duplicate charge flagged by ST-007 (A3), when processed, then a refund is sent through the gateway adapter and the ledger records it.
- Given a chargeback webhook on a deposit (A4), when it arrives, then the related lock is frozen, the user's bidding is suspended, and a Finance case is opened.
- Given a chargeback after a default, when Finance disputes it, then the case shows the forfeiture evidence: T&C version, acceptance time, IP and device (A4).
- Given a forfeiture reversal, when Finance requests it with a reason, then a Super Admin must approve; on approval the amount moves from the revenue account back to the user's Available (PRD 8.1.4).
- Given the forfeiture log in AD-11, when opened, then every forfeiture and reversal is listed with reasons.
- Given every action, when done, then it is in `audit_logs`.
