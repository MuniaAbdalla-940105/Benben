## Story
As a seller (through Ops in the MVP), I want clear choices when the reserve is not met, nobody bids or the chain of offers fails, so that my property is either sold or relisted quickly.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given RESERVE_NOT_MET (A9), when it starts, then the seller has 24 h (SELLER_DECISION) to accept the highest bid; the top bidder's lock is held.
- Given the seller accepts, when recorded (by Ops in the MVP, Decision Q-68), then the auction becomes AWAITING_WINNER and the 48 h starts at acceptance. Assumption (Q-28).
- Given the seller rejects or 24 h pass, when it happens, then the auction becomes RELISTED and all locks are released.
- Given NO_BIDS (A8), when recorded, then the seller is offered relist with a lower start price or move to Marketplace.
- Given relist, when chosen, then a new auction (DRAFT, linked to the parent) is created with prefilled data. Assumption (Q-19).
- Given move to Marketplace, when chosen, then a Marketplace listing draft is created for the property and the auction becomes MOVED_TO_MARKETPLACE. Assumption (Q-19).
- Given the seller withdraws after a valid win (A11), when Ops records it, then the seller commitment lock is forfeited, the winner's lock is fully released, the winner gets a goodwill credit to Available (Assumption Q-16: configurable fixed amount), and the seller is blocked from auctions.
- Given every outcome, when it happens, then participants are notified and events logged.
